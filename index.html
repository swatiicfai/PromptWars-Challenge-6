The Winter Inversion Cycle

During winter nights, the ground cools rapidly, trapping a stagnant layer of cold air beneath warmer atmospheric air. This acts like a giant lid, concentrating vehicular soot and smoke directly where families breathe.

Optimal Ventilation Window:
Between 12:30 PM and 3:30 PM, solar warmth breaks the thermal inversion lid, providing the safest window for brief ventilation and travel.

Child & Family Smog Defense Routine

Target: Households with young children

CRITICAL: Severe smog triggers rapid bronchiolitis in young lungs. Seal nurseries.

🌅

Morning Inversion Peak (06:00 - 09:30)

Keep windows sealed. Do not send children for open-ground assembly. Stroller cover / child-fit N95 for school drop-offs.

☀️

Midday Convective Dip (12:00 - 15:00)

Convective window open. Run 15-minute cross-ventilation only if indoor PM2.5 exceeds outdoor levels. Run Corsi-Rosenthal filter.

🌇

Evening Rush & Cooking Prep (17:00 - 20:30)

Switch cooking to induction or use high-powered range hood to avoid indoor VOC + PM2.5 co-spikes.

🌙

Night Sealing & Filtration (21:00 - 06:00)

Run bedroom air purifier on low whisper mode. Maintain 40-50% indoor relative humidity to settle airborne dust.
